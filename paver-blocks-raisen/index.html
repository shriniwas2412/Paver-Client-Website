Questions from Raisen

Do you deliver paver blocks to Raisen?

Yes. We deliver to Raisen by truck from our factory in Mandideep, about 50 km away. The delivery date is confirmed with your order and depends on quantity and stock.

How do I get the price of paver blocks in Raisen?

Send an enquiry with your area in square feet, the block, thickness and colour, and your site location in Raisen. Delivery to Raisen is included in the terms we send for your quantity.

Which paver block is best for Raisen colony roads and driveways?

Use 80 mm zig-zag (uni) or I-shape (Behaton) blocks for colony roads and daily car use, 60 mm for light home driveways, and 100 mm where trucks drive. Finish the edges with kerb stones.
