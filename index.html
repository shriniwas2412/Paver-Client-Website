BeforeAfter
How your driveway gets laid
  1. Dig and level

    Soft soil comes out and the ground is shaped to slope gently away from the house.

  2. Build the base

    Crushed stone is spread and compacted in layers. This is what carries the weight.

  3. Spread the sand bed

    About 30 mm of sharp sand, screeded flat, gives every block an even seat.

  4. Lay and cut

    Blocks go down in your pattern, with the border laid first and cut pieces at the edges.

  5. Vibrate and fill joints

    A plate compactor beds the blocks and fine sand is brushed into every joint to lock them.

Most home driveways take two to four days to lay, depending on size and weather.

    FAQ

    Questions people ask us

    Still unsure? Send us an enquiry and our team will answer it.

    How do I get the price of paver blocks in Bhopal?

    Send an enquiry with your area in square feet, the block you like, its thickness and colour, and your site location. Our sales team replies the same working day with factory-direct terms for your quantity, with no dealer in between.

    Which thickness do I need for a car driveway?

    60 mm works for a home driveway with occasional car use. 80 mm is the safer choice for daily cars, SUVs and colony roads. For trucks and forklifts, use 100 mm. The load guide above shows the full build-up.

    How many paver blocks are in one square foot?

    About 4.6 rectangular blocks, 3.9 zig-zag blocks, 3.6 I-shape blocks or 2.3 square blocks per sq ft. Add about 5% for cutting at the edges. The pattern studio and enquiry form work this out for you.

    Do you deliver outside Bhopal?

    Yes. We deliver from our Mandideep factory to Bhopal, Obedullaganj, Raisen, Sehore, Vidisha, Narmadapuram and Indore. The delivery date depends on quantity and stock and is confirmed with your order.

    What are uni pavers and Behaton pavers?

    They are trade names for two interlocking shapes. A uni paver is the zig-zag block: the most popular choice for driveways, parking, petrol pumps and industrial yards because its wavy edges resist braking and turning wheels. A Behaton (I-shape or dumbbell) paver suits society internal roads, footpaths and commercial walkways. Hexagon pavers form a honeycomb that suits wide walkways, parks and plazas.

    Will the colour fade?

    The pigment is mixed into the concrete, so the colour runs through the block rather than sitting on top as paint. All concrete pavers lighten slightly over years of sun; washing them once or twice a year keeps them bright.

    Can I see the blocks before I order?

    Yes. Visit our sales office in MP Nagar, Bhopal, or our factory in Mandideep. Send an enquiry first and we will keep samples of the blocks you want ready, or send sample blocks for your architect or site engineer.

    Are pavers better than a concrete slab for a driveway?

    Pavers let rainwater drain through the joints, do not crack across the whole surface, and can be lifted and re-laid if a pipe or cable needs work. A cracked slab has to be broken out.
