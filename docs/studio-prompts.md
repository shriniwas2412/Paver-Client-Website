# Studio hero prompts: premium product shots for Satyam Pavers

## How to use

1. New Gemini chat **per image**. Attach the listed reference photo **first**, then paste the prompt.
2. The block shape must stay **identical to the photo**.
3. Iterate with **one change per follow-up**, always ending: *"Keep everything else exactly the same."*
4. Ask for **4:3 for product cards** and **16:9 for banners**.
5. Download at **full size** and save as the filename given.

Blocks are 60/80 mm thick: a ~10 mm coloured top layer over a light-grey body.

## Fix-up lines (same chat)

- **Shape drift:** "Make the block exactly the outline in my photo: [two V-notches per long side / capital-I with waisted middle / three concave sides]. Keep everything else exactly the same."
- **Plastic or CGI look:** "Make it a real photograph of real concrete: visible pores, sand grains, slightly worn chamfers, imperfect colour. No glossy or 3D-render look. Keep everything else exactly the same."
- **Wrong colour:** "Make the face colour match my photo: muted brick-red, not orange or pink, with a grey concrete body on the sides. Keep everything else exactly the same."
- **Too many blocks:** "Show only [one / three] block(s). Remove the others. Keep everything else exactly the same."
- **Text or logos:** "Remove all text, numbers, logos and watermarks. Keep everything else exactly the same."
