# Competitor review — paver blocks, Bhopal & nearby (Nov 2026)

Pages reviewed: IndiaMART Bhopal "interlocking block" category (~10 sellers), the IndiaMART page of a Bhopal seller, TradeIndia's Bhopal paving listing (43 products), and an Indore manufacturer's blog. The kerbstone.in page didn't load. National brand sites haven't been benchmarked yet.

## What the market looks like
- **No strong local website.** Bhopal sellers sell mostly through IndiaMART/TradeIndia/Justdial pages: one-line descriptions, 1–2 reviews, catalogues padded with unrelated items, typos. Several "Bhopal" listings actually come from sellers in Indore, Delhi or Pune.
- **Everyone shows prices** (mixed units: per piece, per sq ft). We deliberately don't; our pages answer "how do I get the price" and lead straight to an enquiry.
- **No one cites IS 15658, publishes specs, or mentions Mandideep.** No load guides, laying guides or block calculators.
- Claims seen elsewhere ("working life 100 years", unrelated HSN codes) are unverifiable. We avoid that kind of claim.
