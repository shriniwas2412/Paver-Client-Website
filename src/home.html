BeforeAfter
How your driveway gets laid
  1. Dig and level

    Soft soil comes out and the ground is shaped to slope gently away from the house.

  2. Build the base

    Crushed stone is spread and compacted in layers. This is what carries the weight.

  3. Spread the sand bed

    About 30 mm of sharp sand, screeded flat, gives every block an even seat.

  4. Lay and cut

    Blocks go down in your pattern, with the border laid first and cut pieces at the edges.

  5. Vibrate and fill joints

    A plate compactor beds the blocks and fine sand is brushed into every joint to lock them.

Most home driveways take two to four days to lay, depending on size and weather.
