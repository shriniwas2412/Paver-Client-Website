Paver block price in Bhopal: what decides the cost

The price of paver blocks in Bhopal depends on six things: thickness, shape, colour, surface finish, quantity and delivery distance. Laying is a separate cost from supply. Send us your area, block, thickness, colour and site location, and we reply the same working day with factory-direct terms for your order.

  1. Thickness

    Thickness moves the price most, because it sets how much concrete goes into each block. 60 mm costs least, then 80 mm, then 100 mm. Buy the thickness your load needs and no more: 60 mm for paths and light driveways, 80 mm for daily cars and colony roads, 100 mm for trucks and forklifts.

  2. Shape and coverage

    Quotes come per block or per square foot. Always compare per square foot: a zig-zag block covers more ground than a rectangular one, so you need fewer of them (about 3.9 against 4.6 per sq ft).

  3. Colour and finish

    Grey blocks have no added pigment and are usually the lowest-priced. Red, yellow, black and other colours have pigment mixed through the block, which adds to the cost. Decorative faces such as slate, cobble, speckled and anti-skid cost more than plain blocks.

  4. Quantity

    Larger orders get better terms, because making and delivering by the full truckload spreads the cost over more blocks. Builders paving a whole colony can fix terms once for phased delivery.

  5. Delivery distance and site access

    Our factory is in Mandideep, about 25 km from Bhopal. Sites in Bhopal, Mandideep and Obedullaganj are the shortest runs; Raisen, Sehore, Vidisha, Narmadapuram and Indore are further. Narrow lanes where a truck cannot reach the site add handling.

  6. Supply and laying are separate

    The supply price covers blocks delivered to your site. Laying adds labour, sand, the stone sub-base, kerb stones and compaction, and depends on how much digging your site needs. When you compare quotes, compare supply with supply and laying with laying.

  7. Watch out for very cheap blocks

    A block much cheaper than the rest is often thinner than stated, cured for too short a time or low in cement. Check the thickness with a tape, look for clean, sharp edges and even colour, and ask which strength grade it is made to.

  8. Get an exact rate in one message

    Send the area in square feet, the block (or what will drive on it), thickness, colour, site location and when you need it. That is everything we need to quote. Use the enquiry form below or WhatsApp.

Questions

What is the price of paver blocks per sq ft in Bhopal?

It depends on thickness, shape, colour, finish, quantity and site location, so we quote for each order. Send those details and we reply with factory-direct terms the same working day.

Are grey paver blocks cheaper than coloured ones?

Usually, yes. Grey blocks have no added pigment; red, yellow and black blocks carry the cost of the pigment mixed through them.

Is delivery included in the price?

The terms we send are for delivery to your site location, so you can compare them directly with other quotes.

Why buy from the factory instead of a dealer?

We make the blocks at our own Mandideep plant and sell from our MP Nagar office, with no dealer margin in between. Your order comes from matched batches, so the shade stays even across the floor.
