Heavy-duty paver blocks for factory yards

For factory yards, warehouses and loading bays, use 100 mm I-shape (Behaton) interlocking blocks, grade M50 where forklifts and containers run, on a 250–300 mm compacted stone base. 100 mm zig-zag suits truck roads. Pavers take point loads from forklift wheels and container legs, and can be lifted when underground services need work.

Forklifts and point loads

Forklift wheels and container legs press on a small area. A 100 mm M50 block on a well-compacted 300 mm base spreads that load; a thinner block or a weak base lets it punch down and rock.

Plan the yard in zones

Pave forklift aisles and loading bays in 100 mm M50, truck roads in 100 mm, and staff walkways in 60 mm. Zoning keeps the cost in the places that carry the load.

Questions

Which paver block for forklifts?

100 mm I-shape (Behaton) blocks in M50 grade on a 300 mm compacted stone base. Ask your site engineer to confirm the base for your soil.

Are paver blocks better than a concrete floor for a yard?

For outdoor yards, usually yes: pavers do not crack across the whole surface, drain at the joints, and can be lifted to reach cables and pipes. Indoor warehouse floors that need to be dead flat for racking are usually concrete.

How are the blocks checked?

Sample blocks are checked for size, strength and water absorption before dispatch. Ask us for the details for the grade you order.

Can you supply a large yard on a schedule?

Yes. Send the area and the dates, and we make to schedule and deliver by the truckload as each zone is ready.
