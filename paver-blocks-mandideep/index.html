How our blocks are made

Cement, stone dust, grit and pigment are weighed for every batch. Vibration and pressure pack the mix into steel moulds, the blocks are kept moist while they cure, and sample blocks are checked for size, strength and water absorption before dispatch.

How to reach the factory

Mandideep is about 25 km south-east of Bhopal on NH-46, the Bhopal–Narmadapuram road, past Misrod. Send an enquiry before you visit so samples of the blocks you want are ready and the right person is at the gate.

Delivery from Mandideep

Obedullaganj is about 12 km away, Bhopal 25 km, Raisen 50 km, Narmadapuram 55 km, Sehore 65 km, Vidisha 85 km and Indore 215 km. Dispatch dates are confirmed with each order.
