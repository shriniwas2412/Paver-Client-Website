Rectangular Paver Blocks

Rectangular paver blocks are 200 × 100 mm concrete blocks made in 60, 80 and 100 mm thickness. Laid in herringbone they handle cars and turning wheels; laid in running bond or basket weave they suit paths and courtyards. About 4.6 blocks cover one square foot.

Classic and versatile. Herringbone for driveways that take turning cars, running bond for paths, basket weave for courtyards.

FAQ

Rectangular questions

What thickness of rectangular should I use?

Rectangular Paver Blocks come in 60 / 80 / 100 mm. Use the thinnest option for foot traffic, the middle one for cars, and 100 mm where trucks or forklifts drive. Our team confirms the right grade for your site.

How many rectangular blocks do I need?

Plan on about 4.6 blocks per square foot, plus about 5% for cutting at the edges. For 1,000 sq ft that is roughly 4,830 blocks.

Which colours are available?

Rectangular Paver Blocks are made in grey, red, yellow, orange. Ask us about other colours for larger orders.

Do you deliver rectangular to my site?

Yes. We deliver from our Mandideep factory across Bhopal, Obedullaganj, Raisen, Sehore, Vidisha, Narmadapuram and Indore. The delivery date is confirmed with your order.
